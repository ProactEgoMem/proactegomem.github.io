The idea · proactive memory

The Proactive Paradigm

Today's assistants are reactive: they answer when spoken to and stay silent otherwise. But what you will want to know later is often gone from view by the time you ask. A proactive assistant watches your first-person stream in the background, decides on its own when something matters, and keeps it in memory before it leaves the frame.

Streaming egocentric It watches

Your view, one frame at a time. No rewinding and no looking ahead.

Proactive It decides

Nobody prompts it. It picks the moment to speak or act, and stays quiet otherwise.

Co-existent It remembers

It starts, keeps and stops memory tools in the background while you carry on.

Results · Daily

Even the strongest models miss the moment

Across proprietary, open-source, video-language, and streaming baselines, memory-backed accuracy averages 47.6 / 23.6 / 24.2% on IC / ST / LT — yet handing the same models oracle memory lifts them to 76.5 / 49.3 / 57.6%, averaged over all thirteen. Acquiring the right memory, not answering from it, is the bottleneck.

The dataset

Grounded in real first-person activity

Two parallel tracks, one pipeline. ProactEgoMem-Daily is 300 Ego4D clips of everyday first-person activity across 39 scenes, from cooking and laundry to carpentry and car repair. ProactEgoMem-Sci is 100 laboratory clips. Annotators correct, delete or add events on the real timeline, and every question is reviewed by at least three annotators. All annotations and QA are new, so none of it can have leaked into pre-training data.
